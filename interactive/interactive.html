01 · Augmented Reality

¿Quién fue en el 60?

Project Direction · Buenos Aires · 2024–2025

Unity Vuforia SDK Photogrammetry AR Interactive Design

An augmented reality investigation game where the player, acting as a bus line employee, must identify passengers who have underpaid their fares. With only fragmented clues, the player must reconstruct the puzzle and decide who is responsible, knowing that each mistake reduces their chances. The experience unfolds through a hybrid system combining physical evidence and tablet-based augmented reality. Physical objects were digitized using photogrammetry and implemented as recognition targets within the Vuforia SDK, enabling real-time activation of audiovisual content upon detection. Set within the urban landscape of Buenos Aires, the project explores moral ambiguity and the construction of guilt under conditions of incomplete information.

Role

Project direction and visual concept; narrative design and interactive logic development; audiovisual design and sound composition; trailer conception and production.

02 · Real-Time Data Visualization
Work in progress · 2026

Lumina

Concept & Development · TouchDesigner · Biometric Data · Real-Time Projection

Lumina transforms invisible biometric sleep data into contemplative nocturnal landscapes, recovering the ancestral relationship between the human body, the night sky, and the act of sleeping. LUNALOG converts the physiological data from your sleep into a unique starry sky: a visual and poetic archive of your nights, organized into a personal calendar. For users with Apple Watch / Wear OS with real-time streaming: the starry sky is projected onto the ceiling of the bedroom while sleeping, changing in real time according to the physiological data of the moment. The stars change in shape, color, intensity and size depending on the sleep stage of the user.

3D Modeling · Animation · Sound Design · 2024 · 1:25 min

Short 3D animated film set in an amusement park inspired by The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari and German Expressionism. Developed entirely in Blender, focusing on environment modeling, stylized architecture, lighting design and mood construction within a unified visual language.

Generative AI · After Effects

Prefiero ser unx cyborg que una diosa

NVIDIA GauGAN · After Effects · Python · 2020 · 1 min

Monochannel video inspired by Donna Haraway's Cyborg Manifesto (1985). 3D maps created in After Effects are fed into NVIDIA GauGAN and processed via a Python script to generate landscapes automatically through neural networks. An early exploration of generative AI tools applied to feminist posthumanist thinking.
